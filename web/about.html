About us

We are doing this the way we wish somebody had done it for us. Properly, patiently and within the boundaries our deen sets.

Why we started

We’ve been through it! The highs and the lows. The samosas and the pakoras. The awkward silences, ohhhh the awkward silences.

Anyone who has looked for a spouse this way knows how it usually goes. A profile passed around a family group chat. A meeting nobody prepared either side for. Weeks of silence afterwards because neither family wants to be the one to say no. It works out for some people and it leaves plenty of others tired and no closer.

The alternative most people are offered is an app, where you are on your own with a stranger and there is nothing halal about how it is set up. We did not think those were the only two options.

Creating a unique matchmaking experience, while adhering to Islamic principles

Doing it the halal way

Islamic principles are not something we added on top. They are the reason the service is built the way it is. In practice that means four things.

  • Members never contact each other. Not at the start, not after a meeting, not at any point. No numbers are exchanged, no messages are passed on and nobody gets your surname or your address from us. Everything goes through the team.
  • Calls are female to female and male to male. Every member has a proper conversation with us before we start searching, and it is always with someone of the same gender.
  • Meetings are supervised. A neutral public place, a time we arrange with both sides separately, and you may bring someone along. Most people do.
  • Your family is part of this. The form asks for a parent or guardian if you want to give one, and nothing about the way we work asks you to keep this from them.

What a supervised meeting actually means

We choose a neutral public place and we arrange the time with both sides separately. Either of you may bring someone along, and most people do. A parent, a sibling, whoever you are comfortable with.

Nobody is left to arrange anything between themselves. You do not exchange numbers at the meeting and you do not need to. If you want to meet again, you tell us and we set it up.

What we charge, and why

We would rather tell you this here than leave you to find it in the small print.

The £35 is charged once we have found you a match. Not when you sign up and not while we are searching. It covers the following six months, and if you are still with us at the end of that it is another £35.

The money goes back into the service. Matchmaking done properly takes real time. Reading profiles, making calls, thinking carefully about who actually suits who and arranging meetings that work for both families. We would rather charge a small fee and get it right than do it quickly and get it wrong.

That is the whole of it. There is nothing else to pay and nothing hidden underneath it.

When it does not work out

Most matches do not lead to marriage, and that is normal. It is worth saying plainly, because the fear of being turned down is the thing that stops a lot of people starting at all.

You tell us where you stand and we take it from there. You are never told what the other person decided. Only the team sees both answers, so nobody is left feeling turned down. Once a match is closed we start looking again, and the two of you will not be put forward to each other a second time.
