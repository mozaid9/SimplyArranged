Who we are

What the service is

We are a human led matchmaking service, not a dating app. The team reads your profile, searches for someone suitable, presents the match to you and arranges a supervised meeting if you both agree.

The single rule that shapes everything else is that members never contact each other directly. Everything runs through us. We do not pass on your phone number, your email address, your surname or your exact address to anyone.

Who can sign up

To use the service you must be:

  • A British passport holder
  • 18 or over
  • Free to marry
  • Honest in what you tell us

We work mostly across the North West of England. If you are elsewhere in the UK, say so on the form and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.

The fee

£35 for six months.

  • Your subscription begins as soon as we have found a strong potential match and presented it to you. Nothing is charged when you sign up and nothing is charged while we are searching
  • It runs for six months from that first match being presented
  • If you have not found someone by the end of the six months, you can choose to extend

There are two reasons for the fee. It helps make sure that everyone registering is serious and fully committed to their search, and it covers some of our day to day running costs.
