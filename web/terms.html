Terms of service

Last updated 27 July 2026. Version 2026-07-27.1.

These are the terms you agree to when you sign up with Simply Arranged. Plain language on purpose. If anything here is unclear, ask us before you sign up rather than after.

Not ready to publish. This is a working draft. The registered business details, the refund position, the minimum age and whether a second match inside the same six months costs another fee are all still outstanding, and it needs a read by someone qualified before it goes live.

Who we are

Simply Arranged, a matchmaking service. Email simplyarranged@hotmail.com or WhatsApp 07471 881952.

Needed here: registered business name, company number if there is one and a postal address.

What the service is

We are a human led matchmaking service, not a dating app. The team reads your profile, searches for someone suitable, presents the match to you and arranges a supervised meeting if you both agree.

The single rule that shapes everything else is that members never contact each other directly. Everything runs through us. We do not pass on your phone number, your email address, your surname or your exact address to anyone.

Who can sign up

To use the service you must be:

  • A British passport holder
  • 18 or over
  • Free to marry
  • Honest in what you tell us

We work mostly across the North West of England. If you are elsewhere in the UK, say so on the form and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.

To confirm with the client: the minimum age, and whether people outside the North West are accepted or waitlisted.

What we promise, and what we do not

We promise to look properly, to be straight with you and to treat what you tell us with care.

We cannot promise to find you a match, and we cannot promise that a match will lead to marriage. Nobody can. We also cannot verify everything a member tells us about themselves, so please use your own judgement and involve your family.

The fee

A £35 admin fee falls due at the match found stage. Nothing is charged when you sign up and nothing is charged while we are searching.

The fee covers the six months that follow. If you are still with us at the end of those six months, it is another £35 for the next six.

The money is put back into running the service.

Still open: whether the fee is refundable and in what circumstances, and whether a second match inside the same six months is covered by the original fee. The process page currently reads as though it is.

Meetings

If both of you want to go ahead, we arrange a supervised meeting at a neutral location. Either of you may bring someone along. We contact you within three days afterwards to talk about how you want to proceed, and further meetings can be arranged.

You are responsible for your own safety and your own travel. Tell someone where you are going. If anything at a meeting makes you uncomfortable, tell us straight away.

One match at a time

While a match is live you are not put forward to anyone else, and neither is the other person. You go back into the pool when the team closes the match. If a match does not work out, the two of you will not be put forward to each other again.

Neither side is told what the other decided. Only the team sees both answers.

How you must behave

  • Be truthful on your form and on your call
  • Tell us if your circumstances change, particularly if you are no longer free to marry
  • Do not try to contact another member directly or ask us to pass on contact details
  • Do not share anything we tell you about a potential match with anyone outside your immediate family
  • Treat the team and other members with respect

We can suspend or end your membership if you break these, and we will say why.

Ending it

You can ask us to stop at any time by emailing simplyarranged@hotmail.com. We will close your profile and, if you ask, delete your information as set out in the privacy policy.

Your information

How we handle what you tell us is set out in full in the privacy policy. The short version is that the team sees your full profile, a potential match sees a shortened version with identifying detail removed, and nobody else sees anything.

If something goes wrong

We are responsible for doing our job with reasonable care and skill. We are not responsible for how another member behaves, for what they tell us about themselves or for what happens after an introduction. Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

Changes to these terms

If we change anything that matters we will update the date at the top and tell active members by email.

Law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales and the courts of England and Wales have jurisdiction.
