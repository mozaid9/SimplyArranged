How it works

Five steps, from the form to a supervised meeting. A person does every part of it, not an algorithm.

The Process

  1. 1Start Your Journey

    Complete your profile and have a short call with us

    The form takes about ten minutes. Then a member of the team calls you for a proper conversation about what you are actually looking for, female to female and male to male. The call matters more than the form does.

  2. 2Search Initiated

    Data uploaded and your matchmaking search begins

    Nothing is charged at this stage. We start looking, and we keep looking until we find somebody we genuinely think suits you.

  3. 3Match Found

    A £35 admin fee covers a 6-month subscription, and we will present your match for discussion.

    You are told about the match and given time to think and to talk it over with your family. You are under no obligation to say yes.

  4. 4Meeting Arranged

    If both agree, a supervised meeting at a neutral location will be arranged by us; you may bring someone along.

    We pick the place and the time and we handle the arrangements with both sides separately.

  5. 5Next Steps

    Simply Arranged will contact you within 3 days to discuss how you wish to proceed; further meetings can be arranged.

    We call you, not the other way round. Whatever you decide, you tell us and we handle the rest.

You will never be asked to contact anyone yourself. Everything goes through us.

The Fee

The £35 is charged once we have found you a match. Not when you sign up and not while we are searching. It covers the following six months, and if you are still with us at the end of that it is another £35.

The money goes back into the service. Matchmaking done properly takes real time. Reading profiles, making calls, thinking carefully about who actually suits who and arranging meetings that work for both families. We would rather charge a small fee and get it right than do it quickly and get it wrong.

To confirm with the client: whether a second match inside the same six months is covered by the original fee, which is how this page currently reads, and what the refund position is.

What a supervised meeting means

We choose a neutral public place and we arrange the time with both sides separately. Either of you may bring someone along, and most people do. A parent, a sibling, whoever you are comfortable with.

Nobody is left to arrange anything between themselves. You do not exchange numbers at the meeting and you do not need to. If you want to meet again, you tell us and we set it up.

If it does not work out

Most matches do not lead to marriage, and that is normal. You tell us where you stand and we take it from there.

You are never told what the other person decided. Only the team sees both answers, so nobody is left feeling turned down. Once a match is closed we start looking again, and the two of you will not be put forward to each other a second time.
