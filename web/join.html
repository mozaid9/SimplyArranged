Start your journey

Give yourself twenty to thirty minutes. It asks real questions and it is worth answering them properly. Once it is in, a member of the team will call you for a chat about your profile, female to female and male to male.

Simply Arranged is open to British passport holders. For now we work mostly across the North West of England. If you live elsewhere in the UK, tell us where you are on the form and we will see what we can do.

Before you start

The form asks for personal things, so here is exactly what happens to them.

What we collect

Your name, contact details, age, town, occupation and education. What you are looking for in a spouse. Your ethnicity and family origin. Your school of thought, how you are with your Salaah and your Islamic education. Your height and build. Whether you have children. Whether you have any disabilities. If you choose to give them, a parent or guardian's name and number.

Why we need it

To find you a match and to present that match to you. The team reads your full profile. That is the whole service and there is no way round it.

Who sees it

The Simply Arranged team. Nobody else sees your profile as you wrote it. When we present you to a potential match, they see a shortened version with your surname, your contact details and your exact location taken out. Members never contact each other directly at any point.

Your religion, ethnicity and health

School of thought, how you practise, ethnicity and disability are all special category data under UK data protection law. We can only hold them if you give us clear and specific consent, which is what the first tickbox below is for. You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing simplyarranged@hotmail.com and we will delete what we hold.

Where the form lives

The form itself is a Microsoft Form, so your answers go to Microsoft's servers and then to us. Nothing loads from Microsoft until you tick the boxes and continue, so no cookie of theirs is set before then.

The full detail is in the privacy policy.
