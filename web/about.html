About us

We are doing this the way we wish somebody had done it for us. Properly, patiently and within the boundaries our deen sets.

Why we started

We’ve been through it! The highs and the lows. The samosas and the pakoras. The awkward silences, ohhhh the awkward silences.

Anyone who has looked for a spouse this way knows how it usually goes. A profile passed around a family group chat. A meeting nobody prepared either side for. Weeks of silence afterwards because neither family wants to be the one to say no. It works out for some people and it leaves plenty of others tired and no closer.

The alternative most people are offered is an app, where you are on your own with a stranger and there is nothing halal about how it is set up. We did not think those were the only two options.

Doing it the halal way

Islamic principles are not something we added on top. They are the reason the service is built the way it is. In practice that means four things.

  • Members never contact each other. Not at the start, not after a meeting, not at any point. No numbers are exchanged, no messages are passed on and nobody gets your surname or your address from us. Everything goes through the team.
  • Calls are female to female and male to male. Every member has a proper conversation with us before we start searching, and it is always with someone of the same gender.
  • Meetings are supervised. A neutral public place, a time we arrange with both sides separately, and you may bring someone along. Most people do.
  • Your family is part of this. We want at least one parent or guardian to know you are with us, and ideally to be involved when a match is presented to you and when a meeting is arranged. We know that is not everyone's situation and it is something we can talk through on the phone.
