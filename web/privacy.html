Privacy policy

Last updated 27 July 2026. Version 2026-07-27.1.

Simply Arranged is a matchmaking service. To do the job at all we have to hold personal things about you, including your religion and your ethnicity. This page sets out exactly what we hold, why, who sees it and how to get rid of it.

Not ready to publish. This is a working draft. It needs the registered business details, an ICO registration number, a retention period and a read by someone qualified before it goes live.

What we collect

When you sign up

The sign up form asks for:

  • Contact and identity. Full name, email address, phone number, age, gender, town or city
  • Your circumstances. Marital status, whether you have children, whether you would like more, occupation, employer and industry, highest level of education
  • Your background. Ethnicity, ancestral town and country, main language spoken at home
  • Your faith. Whether you are a born Muslim or a revert, the school of thought you follow, how you are with your Salaah, your Islamic education, and how you describe your Islamic appearance
  • Physical detail. Height and build
  • Health. Whether you have any disabilities. The form only asks yes or no. Anything further is discussed on the phone call
  • What you are looking for. Age range, minimum height, ethnicity, school of thought, Islamic education, whether you would consider a divorcee, someone with children or a revert, and where you would live after marriage
  • Free text. A description of yourself, an interesting fact and a description of the spouse you are hoping for
  • Parent or guardian. Their name, phone number, relationship to you and whether they know you have signed up. All optional

Your photos

After the form we ask you to send two to five recent, unfiltered photos of yourself by WhatsApp to 07471 881952, with your name in the message. Photos should only include you.

Your photos are shown to a potential match only once you have given us permission. They are not attached to your profile and passed round without you knowing.

If you wear niqab, we will try to make sure your photos are only seen by a female member of the team. We will do our utmost, but during the matching process itself we cannot always promise it, and we would rather tell you that than pretend otherwise.

Because photos come in over WhatsApp they sit on that service as well as with us, and WhatsApp is owned by Meta. If you would rather send them another way, email simplyarranged@hotmail.com and we will sort something out.

Afterwards

Notes from your phone call with the team, notes from any match we present, notes from meetings we arrange and any emails or messages between you and us.

If you have given us a parent or guardian's details, we may also speak to them when a match is presented and when a meeting is arranged.

Your religion, ethnicity and health

Your school of thought, how you practise, your Islamic education, your ethnicity and any disability you tell us about are all special category data under UK GDPR. The only thing that allows us to hold them is your explicit consent, which you give by ticking the box on the join page before you reach the form.

You can withdraw that consent whenever you like by emailing simplyarranged@hotmail.com. We will delete what we hold and we will not be able to keep looking for a match for you, because there is nothing left to match on. Withdrawing does not undo anything we did before you withdrew.

Who sees it

  • The Simply Arranged team. The team reads your full profile. That is how the service works
  • A potential match. When we present you to someone, they see a shortened version of your profile. Your surname, your contact details and your exact location are taken out. Members never contact each other directly at any point, so nobody gets your number or your email from us
  • Nobody else. We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising

Companies that process it for us

  • Microsoft. The sign up form is a Microsoft Form, so your answers pass through and are stored on Microsoft's systems
  • Meta. Photos and messages sent to our WhatsApp number pass through WhatsApp, which Meta owns
  • Google. The website loads its two typefaces from Google Fonts, which means Google sees the IP address of anyone visiting the site. It sets no cookie. Google also hosts our introduction video on YouTube, but nothing is requested from them until you press play

What we do not do

We do not run background checks and we do not verify anyone's identity or confirm that what they have told us is true. That is set out in the disclaimer you accept when you sign up and it is repeated in the terms. Use your own judgement and involve your family.

To confirm: whether the Microsoft account holding the form is in the UK or EU region, and whether responses leave the UK. If they do, the transfer basis needs stating here.

How long we keep it

While you are an active member, and for a set period after you leave or ask us to stop.

Needs a decision. Handover section 16 flags this. Pick a period, say it here, then actually enforce it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of everything we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete it
  • Stop or limit what we do with it
  • Hand it over in a portable format

You can also withdraw your consent at any time, as set out above. Email simplyarranged@hotmail.com and we will reply within one month.

Cookies

We set no cookies of our own and we run no analytics.

Two things on the site come from somebody else, and neither loads until you ask for it.

  • The sign up form is embedded from Microsoft and Microsoft does set cookies. It does not load until you have ticked the consent boxes and pressed continue
  • The introduction video sits on YouTube. The home page shows a still image of it and loads nothing from Google until you press play, and even then it uses YouTube's no cookie address

That is why there is no cookie banner. There is nothing to ask you about until you choose it.

Age

This service is for adults looking to marry. Do not sign up if you are under 18.

To confirm with the client, then add the same line to the terms and to the form itself, which currently has no minimum age.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything that matters we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects what you consented to, ask you to consent again.

Complaints

Tell us first and we will try to put it right. If you are still unhappy you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
